# AI-SDLC v0.1

**Status:** Working public baseline  
**Date:** 2026-08-22

Version 0.1 is intentionally not presented as a finished standard.

Its purpose is to establish a coherent baseline that can be challenged and validated through real engineering work.

## Included in v0.1

- Human-owned, AI-augmented operating model
- Four levels of zoom: Landscape, Process, Specification, Playbook
- Six lifecycle groups: Discover, Design, Build, Validate, Release, Operate & Learn
- Human/AI participation model
- Constitutional principles and governance baseline
- Working R0–R4 risk classification
- Major lifecycle gates
- Representative artifact flow
- Initial metrics and evaluation candidates
- Initial agent / implementation-contract direction

## Deliberately unfinished

The following areas require field validation and further design:

- exact R0–R4 classification criteria,
- mandatory controls per risk class,
- complete L3 process contracts,
- production-ready L4 playbooks,
- precise metric definitions and baselines,
- organization-specific adoption patterns,
- evidence from real field experiments.

## v0.1 hypothesis

The central hypothesis to test is:

> **Software teams can safely delegate substantially more execution work to GenAI when humans first establish intent, material decisions, constraints and correctness — and when autonomy is bounded by risk-appropriate independent controls.**

The purpose of v0.1 is to test this hypothesis before adding deeper automation or formalization.
