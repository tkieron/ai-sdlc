# 03.01 Idea & Discovery

> **Baseline v1.** This specification is intentionally tool-agnostic and subject to later evaluation under AI-SDLC Continuous Evaluation & Evolution.

## 1. Intent

### Purpose

The purpose of **Idea & Discovery** is to transform a raw idea, need, problem, production signal or opportunity into a sufficiently understood business or technical problem that can be handed to formal Analysis.

This stage answers primarily:

> **What is happening, who is affected, why does it matter, what outcome is desired, and what do we still not know?**

Discovery MUST focus on understanding the problem rather than prematurely validating a proposed solution.

### Scope

**IN SCOPE**

* capture of the idea, problem or opportunity,
* collection of available context and evidence,
* identification of affected users, actors and stakeholders,
* description of the current state,
* clarification of the desired outcome,
* initial understanding of business value or urgency,
* known constraints and dependencies,
* explicit assumptions and open questions,
* initial risk signals,
* high-level behavioral or acceptance scenarios,
* decision whether the topic is mature enough to enter Analysis.

**OUT OF SCOPE**

* detailed functional specification,
* final acceptance criteria,
* architecture and technology selection,
* implementation decomposition,
* detailed estimation,
* detailed test design,
* implementation-level Definition of Done.

### Inputs

Idea & Discovery MAY begin from any credible source, including:

* business or product request,
* user or support feedback,
* incident or production defect,
* telemetry or KPI signal,
* regulatory or contractual obligation,
* technical debt,
* engineering proposal,
* output from the Production Feedback loop.

AI-SDLC MUST NOT require a specific tool, ticket type or form as a prerequisite for starting Discovery.

### Entry Criteria

The stage MAY start when all of the following are true:

* an identifiable problem, need or opportunity exists,
* at least one credible source of context is available or can be identified,
* a human role exists that can validate the relevance of the topic.

Completeness is not required at entry. Discovery exists to create sufficient clarity.

## 2. Responsibility & Authority

### Human Responsibilities

Humans MUST:

* validate the material meaning of the problem,
* distinguish confirmed facts from assumptions,
* confirm who or what is affected,
* validate the desired business or technical outcome,
* approve the final Problem Statement,
* decide whether Discovery is sufficiently complete to proceed to Analysis,
* accept or reject material assumptions proposed by AI.

The definition of the problem remains human-owned.

### AI Responsibilities / Permitted Role

AI MAY:

* facilitate clarification sessions,
* propose discovery questions,
* consolidate meeting notes and recordings,
* identify contradictions or missing information,
* group assumptions and unknowns,
* draft the Problem Statement,
* propose high-level scenarios,
* analyze available documents and data,
* prepare or update logical documentation artifacts,
* identify potential risks or dependencies.

AI MUST NOT:

* present an assumption as a confirmed fact,
* independently establish the business objective,
* redefine the reporter's intent,
* treat a proposed solution as a confirmed requirement without human acceptance,
* invent missing context in order to make the discovery record appear complete.

> **Working rule:** AI may structure uncertainty. It MUST NOT hide uncertainty.

### Decision Rights

| Material Decision | Human Authority |
| --- | --- |
| Whether the problem/opportunity is worth further work | Business / Product / relevant Sponsor |
| Business or technical objective | Business / Product / accountable Owner |
| Acceptance of material assumptions | Appropriate Human Owner |
| Whether discovery is sufficiently complete | Product / Analyst / Team according to context |
| Whether the topic proceeds to Analysis | Accountable Product / Business Owner |
| Whether risk requires early specialist involvement | Relevant Lead / Security / Compliance / Operations owner |

AI MAY recommend, but MUST NOT approve the gate.

## 3. Execution Contract

### Logical Activities

**Capture Idea / Signal → Collect Existing Context → Understand Current State → Identify Stakeholders / Affected Users → Separate Problem from Proposed Solution → Define Desired Outcome → Capture Constraints & Assumptions → Identify Dependencies / Unknowns / Risks → Draft High-Level Behavioral Scenarios → Human Validation → Ready for Analysis**

This is a logical flow, not a mandatory tool workflow.

### Required Artifacts

At exit, the following logical artifacts MUST exist:

* **Problem Statement**,
* **Discovery Record**.

Depending on risk and complexity, the stage MAY also produce:

* Stakeholder / Context Map,
* Initial Scope / Out of Scope,
* High-Level Acceptance Scenarios,
* Initial Risk Notes,
* Initial Dependency Map,
* Decision Records.

Separate files are not mandatory. The information MAY be distributed across approved systems as long as the logical contract is satisfied.

### Mandatory Artifact Content

#### Problem Statement

The Problem Statement MUST contain, where applicable:

* **Problem / Opportunity** — what is observed,
* **Affected Users / Actors** — who is impacted,
* **Current State** — how the situation works today,
* **Why It Matters** — business or technical significance,
* **Desired Outcome** — expected result,
* **Known Evidence** — facts supporting the problem,
* **Known Constraints**,
* **Assumptions**,
* **Open Questions**.

Facts, assumptions and open questions MUST remain distinguishable.

#### Discovery Record

The Discovery Record SHOULD preserve:

* important collected context,
* sources of information,
* material findings,
* unresolved questions,
* dependencies,
* initial risk signals,
* decisions already made,
* owners of material open questions.

AI MAY perform most of the mechanical preparation and consolidation of this artifact.

## 4. Control

### Primary Gate — Ready for Analysis

Idea & Discovery is complete when there is sufficient understanding to deliberately analyze the problem.

**Ready for Analysis does not mean “we know everything”. It means “we know enough to analyze the problem deliberately”.**

### Exit Criteria

The gate MUST confirm, proportionally to risk, that:

* the problem or opportunity is sufficiently clear,
* the desired outcome is understood,
* affected actors are known where relevant,
* material available context has been collected,
* facts and assumptions are distinguishable,
* key constraints and dependencies are visible,
* critical unknowns are explicit,
* preliminary risk signals are recorded,
* no hidden material contradiction is knowingly ignored,
* the accountable Human Owner approves progression.

### Required Evidence

Minimum evidence SHOULD include:

* approved Problem Statement,
* current Discovery Record,
* visible material assumptions and open questions,
* human approval of the transition to Analysis.

### Risk Adjustments R0–R4

* **R0 — Trivial / Mechanical:** MAY use a lightweight discovery record with problem, outcome, owner and basic success signal.
* **R1 — Standard:** SHOULD use the standard Problem Statement, assumptions, open questions and human approval.
* **R2 — Significant:** SHOULD explicitly capture stakeholders, dependencies, preliminary risk assessment, important negative scenarios and initial NFR concerns.
* **R3 — High Risk:** MUST use more formal Discovery and SHOULD involve relevant Security, Privacy, Data, Operations or Compliance roles early where applicable.
* **R4 — Critical:** MUST NOT proceed without explicit identification of critical constraints, accountable owners and material regulatory/security limitations. AI access MAY be additionally restricted.

### Stop / Escalation Conditions

The stage MUST stop or escalate when:

* no accountable Human Owner can be identified,
* business objectives materially conflict,
* key information is unreliable or contradictory,
* security, privacy, legal or contractual constraints may be violated,
* it is unclear whether data may be exposed to AI,
* material risk classification is uncertain,
* a critical dependency has no accountable owner,
* AI cannot distinguish fact from assumption based on available evidence.

Use: **STOP → DESCRIBE → PROPOSE → ESCALATE**.

### Exceptions

Emergency or incident response MAY use compressed Discovery. At minimum it MUST still preserve:

* the problem,
* current impact,
* accountable owner,
* current risk,
* intended operational outcome.

Missing discovery information SHOULD be reconstructed after stabilization where useful.

## 5. Traceability & Navigation

### Traceability

The logical chain SHOULD remain recoverable:

**Source Signal / Idea → Problem Statement → Discovery Evidence → Assumptions / Open Questions → Ready for Analysis → 03.02 Analysis**

For production-originated work:

**Metric / Incident / Feedback → Idea & Discovery**

### Upstream / Downstream Contracts

**Typical upstream sources:** Business / Product, Support, Incidents, Production Feedback, Metrics, Engineering Team.

**Primary downstream consumer:** **03.02 Analysis**.

Analysis SHOULD receive the Problem Statement, Discovery Record, assumptions, constraints, open questions, high-level scenarios and known risks/dependencies.
