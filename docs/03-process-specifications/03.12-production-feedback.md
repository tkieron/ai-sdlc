# 03.12 Production Feedback

> **Baseline v1.** Production Feedback closes the AI-SDLC loop by converting operational reality into evidence, learning and new work. The phase is continuous rather than a one-time terminal step.

## 1. Intent

### Purpose

The purpose of **Production Feedback** is to observe how the released system behaves in reality, identify meaningful technical and business signals, learn from incidents and user experience, and feed validated insights back into Discovery, backlog improvement and AI-SDLC evolution.

Production is not only the destination of delivery. It is a continuous source of evidence.

### Scope

**IN SCOPE**

* technical telemetry and service-health signals,
* business KPIs and product outcomes,
* incidents and production defects,
* support/user feedback,
* post-release observations,
* correlation of changes with operational behavior,
* lessons learned and improvement proposals,
* identification of new ideas, defects, technical debt or process improvements,
* feedback into AI-SDLC metrics and framework evolution.

**OUT OF SCOPE**

* full Discovery/Analysis of every new idea,
* detailed implementation planning,
* replacing formal incident-management procedures,
* autonomous business prioritization by AI.

### Inputs

Production Feedback MAY consume:

* production metrics, logs and traces,
* business KPIs,
* incidents and alerts,
* support/user reports,
* release and deployment evidence,
* rollback/recovery events,
* known residual risks,
* review/validation history where useful,
* human qualitative observations.

### Entry Criteria

The phase is continuously active for operating systems. A discrete feedback item MAY enter processing whenever a meaningful operational, business or user signal is identified.

## 2. Responsibility & Authority

### Human Responsibilities

Humans MUST:

* decide which signals are materially important,
* validate business meaning and priority,
* distinguish correlation from confirmed causation where decisions are material,
* own incident conclusions and risk acceptance,
* approve backlog/new-idea creation and prioritization,
* decide whether a finding requires process/framework change,
* ensure lessons learned are not reduced to blame attribution.

### AI Responsibilities / Permitted Role

AI MAY:

* aggregate telemetry and feedback,
* correlate release/change history with production signals,
* cluster recurring incidents or support themes,
* summarize incidents and operational evidence,
* identify anomalous trends,
* propose hypotheses and improvement candidates,
* draft lessons learned and backlog candidates,
* analyze patterns in AI-assisted failures, rework and review outcomes.

AI MUST NOT:

* present correlation as proven causation without evidence,
* autonomously assign business priority,
* hide uncertainty in incident/root-cause analysis,
* alter production evidence to fit a preferred narrative,
* become the sole authority for incident or postmortem conclusions.

### Decision Rights

| Material Decision | Human Authority |
| --- | --- |
| Incident severity / business significance | Incident / Operations / Business authority |
| Root-cause acceptance | Engineering / Incident Owner |
| Whether signal becomes backlog/new idea | Product / Engineering Owner |
| Priority of new work | Product / Business / Engineering governance |
| AI-SDLC process change | Framework / Engineering governance |
| Risk-control recalibration | Appropriate Risk / Engineering Owner |

## 3. Execution Contract

### Logical Activities

**Collect Production Signals → Normalize / Correlate Evidence → Identify Material Patterns → Human Validate Meaning → Capture Incident / Feedback Findings → Derive Lessons Learned → Create Improvement / New Work Candidate → Route to Discovery / Framework Evaluation → Monitor Outcome**

### Required Artifacts

For material feedback, logical outputs SHOULD include, where applicable:

* **Operational Evidence / Signal Record**,
* **Incident / Defect / Feedback Record**,
* **Lessons Learned**,
* **Improvement Proposal / New Work Item**,
* **AI-SDLC Improvement Candidate** where the process itself contributed to the outcome.

### Mandatory Artifact Content

Material feedback records SHOULD capture:

* signal/source,
* affected system/business area,
* observed impact,
* relevant release/change context,
* known evidence,
* assumptions/hypotheses,
* confirmed findings,
* responsible owner,
* decision or follow-up action,
* link to new Discovery or improvement work where applicable.

Incident learning SHOULD ask not only **“who approved this?”** but also **“what in the engineering system allowed this outcome to occur?”**.

## 4. Control

### Local Gate — Signal Triaged / Learning Captured

Because Production Feedback is continuous, it does not end with one global gate. Each material signal SHOULD reach a state where its meaning, owner and next action are sufficiently clear.

### Exit Criteria for a Material Feedback Item

A material signal SHOULD be considered processed when:

* the evidence source is identifiable,
* impact is understood sufficiently for action,
* assumptions and confirmed facts are distinguishable,
* an accountable owner exists,
* required incident/risk decisions have been made,
* follow-up action is explicit,
* relevant new work is routed to Discovery/backlog,
* framework/process learning is captured where applicable.

### Required Evidence

Evidence MAY include:

* telemetry snapshots/dashboards,
* incident records,
* support/user feedback,
* release/change correlation,
* postmortem or learning notes,
* created improvement/backlog item,
* AI-SDLC decision/change record.

### Risk Adjustments R0–R4

* **R0:** MAY use lightweight operational feedback and normal backlog capture.
* **R1:** SHOULD use standard telemetry/defect/feedback triage.
* **R2:** SHOULD preserve explicit release correlation, impact and lessons learned for material issues.
* **R3:** MUST use formal incident/learning evidence for serious production failures and SHOULD evaluate whether controls were sufficient.
* **R4:** MUST preserve auditable operational evidence, explicit specialist conclusions and governance review where critical safety, security, financial, regulatory or data-integrity outcomes are involved.

### Stop / Escalation Conditions

Escalate immediately when:

* production safety/security/data-integrity risk is active,
* incident impact is increasing,
* root cause remains uncertain while destructive remediation is proposed,
* AI recommends an action outside its production authority,
* evidence suggests systemic failure of a required AI-SDLC control,
* regulatory/contractual reporting obligations may apply.

### Exceptions

During active incidents, restoration may take priority over complete documentation. Minimum ownership, evidence preservation and decision traceability MUST still be maintained, and missing learning records SHOULD be completed after stabilization.

## 5. Traceability & Navigation

### Traceability

**Release Accepted → Production Evidence / Incident / Feedback → Lessons Learned → Improvement / New Idea → 03.01 Idea & Discovery**

For framework learning:

**AI-assisted Outcome → Metrics / Incident / Rework Evidence → AI-SDLC Evaluation → Decision Record → Framework Change**

### Upstream / Downstream Contracts

**Consumes:** 03.11 Release & Deployment plus ongoing operations/support/business evidence.

**Produces for:** 03.01 Idea & Discovery, backlog/product management, 06 Metrics & Evaluation, 07 Change Log & Decision Records.
