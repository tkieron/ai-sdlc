# 03.05 Planning

> **Baseline v1.** Planning is predominantly human-owned. AI may automate mechanical work-item creation and consistency checks after the plan is defined.

## 1. Intent

### Purpose

The purpose of **Planning** is to transform refined and approved intent into a coherent delivery plan that the team understands, can sequence and can execute without delegating material planning decisions to AI.

Planning determines **how the work is divided and coordinated**, not how individual methods or classes are implemented.

### Scope

**IN SCOPE**

* decomposition into deliverable work units,
* sequencing and dependency ordering,
* estimation or sizing,
* ownership and coordination assumptions,
* definition of implementation-level Definition of Done,
* identification of cross-team or release dependencies,
* validation that the planned work covers the approved scope,
* identification of risks introduced by decomposition or sequencing.

**OUT OF SCOPE**

* class/method implementation design,
* final test implementation,
* agent execution details,
* architecture decisions not already owned by an authorized human.

### Inputs

Planning SHOULD consume:

* refined requirements,
* approved architecture and decision records,
* current acceptance criteria,
* known risks and dependencies,
* current risk classification,
* delivery constraints and team capacity context where relevant.

### Entry Criteria

Planning MAY start when:

* Refinement is complete enough for meaningful decomposition,
* material architecture constraints are known,
* blocking questions have owners or are resolved,
* the team has sufficient context to estimate and sequence work without inventing requirements.

## 2. Responsibility & Authority

### Human Responsibilities

Humans MUST:

* own decomposition decisions,
* decide sequencing and delivery strategy,
* estimate or size the work,
* ensure planned items collectively cover the approved scope,
* define or approve Definition of Done,
* identify critical dependencies and delivery risks,
* approve the final plan.

### AI Responsibilities / Permitted Role

AI MAY:

* propose decomposition options for human review,
* check whether planned items appear to cover known requirements,
* identify missing dependencies or duplicated scope,
* generate draft work items/subtasks from an approved plan,
* populate mechanical metadata,
* create draft descriptions and links between logical artifacts,
* summarize planning decisions.

AI MUST NOT:

* independently commit the team to estimates,
* redefine scope to fit a decomposition,
* approve Definition of Done,
* silently convert uncertain work into a lower-risk or simpler plan,
* make Material Decisions about architecture, contracts or business behavior.

### Decision Rights

| Material Decision | Human Authority |
| --- | --- |
| Decomposition into work units | Team / Tech Lead |
| Sequencing and dependency strategy | Team / Lead / Delivery Owner |
| Estimates / sizing | Delivery Team |
| Definition of Done | Team / Lead / Product as applicable |
| Cross-team delivery commitment | Relevant Delivery Owners |
| Plan approval | Team / Lead / Product according to local governance |

## 3. Execution Contract

### Logical Activities

**Review Refined Scope → Identify Deliverable Work Units → Define Dependencies & Sequence → Estimate / Size → Define DoD → Validate Coverage Against Requirements → Reassess Delivery Risk → Human Approval → Planned Work Ready for Detailed Design**

### Required Artifacts

At exit, logical artifacts MUST include:

* **Work Breakdown / Delivery Plan**,
* **Definition of Done**,
* **Dependency / Sequencing Information**.

Depending on context, Planning MAY also produce milestone or release grouping, ownership assumptions, capacity notes, cross-team coordination items and explicit risk notes.

### Mandatory Artifact Content

The Work Breakdown MUST make clear, where applicable:

* the deliverable units of work,
* relationship to approved requirements,
* dependency order,
* relevant owners or responsible roles,
* estimation/sizing evidence,
* completion expectations,
* known coordination constraints,
* residual delivery risks.

The Definition of Done MUST be explicit enough to support later Test Design, Implementation Design, Review and Acceptance.

## 4. Control

### Local Gate — Plan Approved / Ready for Detailed Design

Planning is complete when the team can explain how the approved scope will be delivered and when downstream Test Design and Implementation Design can proceed without re-planning hidden requirements.

### Exit Criteria

The gate SHOULD confirm, proportionally to risk, that:

* the approved scope is covered by planned work,
* work units are understandable and appropriately sized,
* dependencies and sequencing are visible,
* estimates/sizing are human-owned,
* Definition of Done exists,
* cross-team dependencies have owners,
* material delivery risks are known,
* no planning artifact silently changes functional or architectural intent.

### Required Evidence

Evidence MAY include:

* approved work breakdown,
* agreed estimates/sizing,
* Definition of Done,
* dependency map or equivalent logical record,
* planning decision summary.

### Risk Adjustments R0–R4

* **R0:** MAY use a single lightweight work item and simplified DoD.
* **R1:** SHOULD use standard decomposition and team-owned estimates.
* **R2:** SHOULD explicitly capture sequencing, cross-component dependencies and risk-relevant DoD items.
* **R3:** MUST make critical dependencies, owners and release constraints explicit and SHOULD require lead approval.
* **R4:** MUST use deliberate human planning, explicit coordination and auditable completion criteria; broad autonomous AI planning is not appropriate.

### Stop / Escalation Conditions

Escalate when:

* the scope cannot be decomposed without changing requirements,
* estimates are impossible because material uncertainty remains,
* a dependency has no owner,
* planning reveals a new Material Decision,
* risk class increases materially,
* Definition of Done cannot be stated because correctness is still unclear.

### Exceptions

Emergency work MAY use abbreviated planning, but MUST still preserve accountable ownership, immediate objective, minimum DoD, major dependencies and recovery considerations where production is affected.

### Traceability

**Approved Requirement / Architecture → Planned Work Unit → Definition of Done → Test Design / Implementation Design**
